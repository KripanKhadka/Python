Overload

Training load exceeds athletes capacity

Physical Symptoms
  • Chronic fatigue
  • Muscle Soreness
  • Decrease Performance
  • Injury Risk
Physiological Symptoms
  • Elevated heart Rate
  • Sleep Disturbance
  • Poor Appetite
  • Hormonal Imbalance
Psychological Symptoms
  • Lack Of Motivation
  • Depression
  • Irritability
  • Anxiety

Prevention From Overload
1) Periodization:
  • Proper cyclic training (rest & training)
2) Monitoring Load:
  • Training log book - heart rate, Blood pressure
3) Recovery Process:
  • Massage, Stretching, Hydrotherapy, Proper sleep
4) Communication:
  • Open dialogue between coach & athletes (regarding training, diet etc)
5) Cross Training:
  • Mix training method to reduce physical and mental stress
6) Mental Training
  • Use relaxation techniques, Autogenous training, Yoga, Stress Management

Methods of Recovery

Body returns to normal state after training. Necessary for adaptation, performance, improvement.

1) Immediate recovery phase
  • Occurs within a minutes to hour after training or competition
  • Focus on replenishing energy ATP, Glycogen, rehydrating and remove lactic acid
2) Short term recovery
  • Lasts few hours to days, Repair damage tissue, Restore hormonal balance, Reduce muscle soreness
  • Involves active recovery, massage, hydration, light activity
3) Long term Recovery
  • Over days, weeks, month depending on intensity and goals
  • Includes super compensation
  • Important periodized training

Physiological Load
1) Cardiovascular Load
  • Pumps oxygen & nutrients to muscles
  • Increases heart rate
  • Increases stroke volume & output
2) Musculoskeletal Load
  • Impact forces pass through bone
  • Varies by technique & footwear
  • High intensity stresses structure
3) Energy System Load
  • Sustained energy demand
  • Aerobic: oxygen via blood/lungs
  • Anaerobic: produces lactic acid/CO₂
4) Thermoregulatory Load
  • Activity generates internal heat
  • Sweating & blood flow cool body
  • Heat & humidity increases stress
5) Hormonal and Neuroendocrine Load
  • Releases endorphins & cortisol
  • Regulates mood, repair & stress
  • Scales with workout intensity
